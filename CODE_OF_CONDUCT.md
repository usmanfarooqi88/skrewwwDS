# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This project is a small, technical, public design system. Everyone
participating in it — issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews — is
expected to keep interactions respectful and focused on the work.

Expected:

- Assume good faith. Ask what someone meant before assuming the worst reading.
- Critique code, decisions, and evidence — not people.
- Be direct about technical disagreement, and specific about why.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a change for scope or direction reasons.

Not acceptable:

- Harassment, personal attacks, or demeaning comments.
- Discrimination or exclusionary remarks based on any personal characteristic.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, deliberate bad-faith argument, or trolling.
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.

## Scope

Applies in all project spaces (repository, issues, pull requests,
discussions) and when representing the project publicly.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers.

**Open item:** a dedicated contact route for conduct reports has not been
established yet. Until it is, raise concerns through the project's public
issue tracker for anything that can be discussed in the open, and note that a
private channel is still being set up for anything that cannot. This is
tracked as an open launch item in
[`docs/open-source-readiness.md`](docs/open-source-readiness.md) — it is a
real gap, not an oversight to work around.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or delete contributions that violate this policy,
and may temporarily or permanently block accounts for repeated or severe
violations. Enforcement decisions aim to be proportionate and explained where
practical.
